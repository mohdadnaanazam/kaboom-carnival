Help & Support

How do I play?

Drag anywhere to aim the cannon, then let go to fire. You can also tap to fire straight ahead. Clear every jar, can and ice block off the shelves before you run out of shots.

How do stars work?

Clear a level in the fewest shots for 3 stars. Replaying for more stars earns bonus coins.

How do lives work?

You lose a life when you fail a level. Lives refill one every 20 minutes on a real clock, even when the app is closed, or you can refill them with coins.

Are there purchases or ads?

No. Everything is earned with in-game coins.
