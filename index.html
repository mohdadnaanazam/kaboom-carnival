Aim. Fire. Smash.

Kaboom Carnival is a physics cannon game for iPhone. Aim your cannon, fire, and knock jars, cans, ice and TNT off festival shelves across 200 levels in 10 themed worlds.

  • Satisfying 3D physics and shattering glass
  • Boosters: Heavy Ball, Triple Shot, Bomb Ball, Rocket and Laser Aim
  • Daily challenge and daily rewards
  • Fair lives that refill on a real clock
  • Works offline. No ads, no purchases, no data collection
