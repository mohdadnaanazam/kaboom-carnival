Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026

Kaboom Carnival ("the app") is made by Adnaan Azam. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

We collect no data

The app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).

What stays on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters, cannon skins and settings) is saved only on your iPhone using standard iOS storage. It never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also erase it at any time from Settings → Reset progress inside the app.

Network access

The game works fully offline. The only time it uses the internet is when you tap the Privacy Policy or Help links, which open this website in your browser.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
